# Financial-service marks

These local, optimized WebP images identify the existing financial services.
They do not imply a partnership or endorsement. Trademarks remain the property
of their respective owners.

`sources.json` records the exact source and SHA-256 of each asset:

- `official`: obtained from the provider, its official application listing,
  official corporate site, or its own asset CDN.
- `reference`: a reproduction of the brand mark from Wikimedia, a logo
  reference archive, or a business publication when the official asset could
  not be downloaded.

Some images are the official corporate mark rather than a product-specific
mark (for example Airtel, Moov, MTN and Celtiis). NITA's mark is shared by
MyNITA and NITA. The MTN mark is shared across Benin and Ghana.

The original service URLs and country data remain in the existing authoritative
transfer implementation. This directory introduces no external image requests
at runtime. If an image fails to load, the existing card remains an accessible
link and shows a neutral initial-based graphic instead of a broken image.
